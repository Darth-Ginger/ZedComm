# ZedComm crafting recipes

Every recipe is in the crafting menu under Electrical. Recipes marked with a screwdriver need one in the inventory and do not use it up. Electrical is the skill level required; a dash means none.

Wire means the roll of wire, which holds 10 units; amounts of it are in units. It can replace Electric Wire in any recipe at 2 units for each Electric Wire.

This file is written by `tools/make-recipes.py`. Change recipes there, not here.
