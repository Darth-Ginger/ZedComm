The wall-mounted `network-cabinet-closed.png` remains the original concept. The two new closed cabinet sources are a freestanding floor enclosure with a plinth, and a sideways tabletop version of the wall enclosure. All three still need placement anchors and terminal mapping before packing.

The variants were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool using `network-cabinet-closed.png` as the edit reference.

## Tabletop cabinet revision

`network-cabinet-tabletop-v2.png` is the current bracket-free tabletop concept. The earlier sideways wall enclosure remains a design reference. Native-size exports and their provisional mounting points are documented in [Cabinet sprite prototypes](../cabinet-prototypes/README.md).

Both passes used built-in ImageGen. The first used the sideways cabinet as its edit reference. The second used the bench enclosure output.

### Bench enclosure prompt

Redesign this ZedComm tabletop network cabinet as a purpose-built early-1990s industrial bench enclosure, preserving only the muted warm gray powder coat and dark latch vocabulary. This is a new engineered tabletop silhouette, low, wide and shallow like a short horizontal test instrument. Its face is about three times as wide as it is high. Use an uninterrupted rectangular housing with softly beveled sheet-metal corners and two small visible low dark rubber feet directly beneath the near corners, supporting a flat bench footprint. Absolutely no mounting ears, no mounting tabs, no metal brackets, no projecting flanges, no external bolt loops, no holes at the outer edges. Use a broad front service panel with a small recessed dark latch on the right, a subtle seam around the panel, and two short horizontal vent banks or a simple top ventilation bank. Put three small flush/recessed cable sockets in a horizontal bank low on the right side. Large simple parts readable when reduced to 70 pixels wide. View from above at a Project Zomboid-style isometric three-quarter view with upright verticals, showing the top, front and right face. Crisp grounded pixel-art edges, restrained wear, transparent RGBA background. One complete isolated cabinet, no table, floor, scene, shadows beyond the body, hanging wires, text, logo, or alternate view.

### Projection pass prompt
